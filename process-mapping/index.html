Why map before you fix

Fixing one area in isolation often overloads another. A marketing problem turns out to be a sales process problem; a hiring problem turns out to be missing onboarding. A map shows the whole system, so you fix the cause instead of the symptom.

What we map

  • The client journey: how people find you, buy, get onboarded and get served.
  • The employee journey: how people are hired, onboarded, trained and managed.
  • Who owns each step, which tools it runs in, and where work waits on one person.

Where mapping fits in the framework

Mapping is how we run the Dump step of Dump, Document, Delegate. The map is the Dump, made visible. Then we Optimize each step: eliminate, consolidate, delegate or automate. What stays gets documented as SOPs and handed to the right person or tool.

What you walk away with

A map of how your operation runs today, the gaps between what is supposed to happen and what does, and a prioritized list of what to fix first. Our Process and Product Management Lead, a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt and certified Project Management Professional, leads the mapping.

Frequently asked questions

What is process mapping?

Process mapping is drawing every step of a workflow, from what starts it to what it produces, including who does each step and where it can stall. It makes work that lives in people's heads visible, so it can be fixed or handed off.

Which processes should a small business map first?

Map first the processes that cause the most rework, the most errors or the most dependence on one person. For most founder-led businesses, that is client onboarding and delivery, and hiring and training.

What is the difference between a process map and an SOP?

A process map shows the whole flow and how the steps connect, while an SOP is the written instruction for doing one process. We map first, then write SOPs for what stays.

Do I need process mapping before automating?

Yes. Automating a process nobody has mapped makes the same mistakes faster, so we map it, fix it, and only then automate it.

How long does process mapping take?

It depends on how many areas are in scope. Mapping is usually the first phase of an Assessment or Acceleration engagement, which typically runs 4 to 12 weeks.
