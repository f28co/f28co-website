Fractional COO

Senior operating leadership, without a full-time salary

A fractional COO is a senior operations leader who runs your operation part time, on a retainer, instead of as a full-time executive hire. At F28 & Co., fractional COO leadership is for businesses whose systems are in place and who now need someone in the seat driving execution, accountability and continuous improvement. If the systems are not in place yet, we start there first.

Do you need a fractional COO or an operations manager?

An operations manager runs the day-to-day inside a system someone has already designed. A fractional COO designs that system and leads it. If your processes are documented and you need reliable execution, hire the manager. If everything still routes through you and nobody has designed how the work should run, you need the COO-level work first. Many of our clients do both: our team builds the operating system, then recruits an operations hire to run it.

Four ways to work with us

These are not a ladder. Start with the one that matches the problem in front of you.

  • Assessment. Something feels off but you cannot name it. We diagnose how the business really runs and hand you a prioritized roadmap.
  • Acceleration. You know the bottleneck. We fix that one operational problem without redesigning everything else. Usually 4 to 12 weeks.
  • Transformation. The business has outgrown how it operates. We redesign the operating system and build the processes and infrastructure to scale or sell. Usually 6 to 18 months.
  • Leadership. Your systems are in place. Fractional COO leadership drives execution and accountability, on an ongoing retainer.

How we work

Every engagement follows Dump, Document, Delegate: we get the work out of the founder's head, decide what to eliminate, consolidate, delegate or automate, document what stays, and hand it to the right people and tools. Fallon Scott, CPA, our founder, holds two fractional COO seats at a time so each client gets real depth. Our team delivers the assessment, documentation, automation and recruiting work around her.

Who it is for

Founder-led businesses where too much still runs through the founder or one or two key people. Most of our clients have 10 to 100 people.

Frequently asked questions

What does a fractional COO do for a small business?

A fractional COO leads your operation part time: setting priorities, holding the team accountable, fixing what breaks and improving how the work runs. At F28, that leadership sits on top of documented processes, clear roles and the right tools, which our team builds first when they are missing.

Do I need a fractional COO or an operations manager?

You need an operations manager if your systems are designed and you need someone to run them day to day, and a fractional COO if nobody has designed how the business should run yet. If every decision still routes through you, start with the COO-level work.

How much does a fractional COO cost?

At F28, fractional COO leadership is an ongoing monthly retainer, scoped after a discovery call to what your business needs rather than sold as a package. You get a scoped proposal, not a number on the first call.

What is the difference between a fractional COO and a full-time COO?

A fractional COO works with you part time on a retainer, while a full-time COO is a permanent executive salary. Fractional fits when you need senior operating leadership before a full-time hire makes sense.

How fast can a fractional COO start?

Our team can usually start within 1 to 2 weeks of an accepted proposal. Fractional COO leadership from Fallon depends on when one of her two seats opens.

When does a business need a fractional COO?

A business needs COO-level help when growth adds chaos instead of margin: every decision waits on the founder, the same fires come back with every new client or hire, and the founder cannot step away for a week without something breaking.
