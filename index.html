Our story

An agency clients wouldn't leave.

F28 Agency started in 2019 building workflows and systems for founders. Clients hired us for one project and kept us for the next: marketing, then operations, then hiring.

Along the way we kept seeing the same pattern. A marketing problem was really a sales process problem. A hiring problem was really an org design problem: no hiring plan, no onboarding or training, and no documented processes to show new hires what to do, which turned into frustration and turnover. And the founder was still holding everything together.

We outgrew the agency model. So did our clients. Today F28 & Co. is an operations consulting firm: we lead operations in-house and bring in vetted specialists where the work calls for it.
