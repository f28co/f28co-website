Dump, Document, Delegate

Dump, Document, Delegate is how F28 & Co. moves work out of a founder's head and into a system a team, a new hire or an AI agent can run. Dump gets every task out of your head and onto one list, then sorts what stays. Document writes it as SOPs. Delegate hands each one to the right person or tool.

Why founders get stuck

Most founders are the operating system. Every answer, approval and fix routes through them, so the business can only move as fast as they can. Hiring help does not fix that on its own. When roles are fuzzy and the know-how lives in your head, every new hire takes longer to ramp and leaves sooner. Dump, Document, Delegate fixes the order of operations.

Step 1. Dump: get it all out of your head

Dump does not mean throwing work away. It means getting every task you and your team do out of your head and onto one list, before you judge any of it. It has three parts:

  • Offload: list every recurring task, decision and handoff, across the client journey and the employee journey, plus HR, finance and admin.
  • Organize: group the tasks by area and by who does them today.
  • Optimize: decide what each task needs. Eliminate it, consolidate it with another, delegate it or automate it, and rank what stays by time saved, risk reduced and cost cut.

Optimize is the judgment step. Software can hold the list, but people make these calls, and it is where our team does most of its work with clients. It keeps you from documenting work that should not be done at all.

Step 2. Document: write it so someone else can run it

We map each process end to end, then write it as an SOP your team can follow without asking you. One SOP template, one masterlist and one place to store them keep everything together, and the SOPs go into the tools your team already uses so they get followed, not filed.

Step 3. Delegate: hand it to the right person or tool

Repetitive, rule-based, high-volume work can go to automation or AI. Judgment, relationships and nuance stay with people, and many roles are a mix of both. Each seat gets clear ownership and decision rights. Before any AI takes on a task, we put governance in place: who approved it, what data it can touch and who reviews its work.

Frequently asked questions

What does Dump mean in Dump, Document, Delegate?

Dump means getting every task out of your head and onto one list: offload it, organize it, then optimize it. Nothing is thrown away while you are getting it out. Deciding what to eliminate, consolidate, delegate or automate comes once it is all on the list.

Is this just a delegation framework?

No, delegation is the last of the three steps. Skip Dump and Document and you hand someone a task nobody has defined, which is why so many first hires end up shadowing the founder for weeks.

Can AI agents handle the Delegate step?

Yes, for repetitive, rule-based, high-volume work, once the process is documented and the AI use is governed. Judgment, relationships and nuance stay with people.

How is this different from just writing SOPs?

Writing SOPs is the middle step. Dump comes first, so you document the right work in the right order instead of writing manuals for tasks that should be eliminated or automated.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. The SOP Suite walks you through Dump and Document on your own, with Delegate as an add-on. If you would rather have our team run it with you, that is what an Operations & Process engagement is for.

How long does it take with F28?

It depends on scope. A targeted Acceleration engagement usually runs 4 to 12 weeks, and a full Transformation runs 6 to 18 months, depending on company size.
